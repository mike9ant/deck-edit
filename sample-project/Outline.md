## Story arc

1. **Laugh** (1–2): the image of coffee floating away does the work.
2. **Believe** (3–6): the market is real, the physics is real (see *Zero-G Brewing — Technical Design.pdf*), the product is delightful.
3. **Trust** (7–10): the team, the numbers, the competition.
4. **Act** (11–14): where it goes, what could go wrong, what we need.
