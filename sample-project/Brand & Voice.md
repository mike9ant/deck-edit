# Rabbit Hole Roasters — Brand & Voice

For anyone (human or agent) adding slides: match this, and the deck stays one deck.

## Voice

- **Playful, never silly about the physics.** The jokes are in the framing; the numbers are real.
- **Short.** A slide title is a sentence that makes the point. Body text: three bullets at most.
- **Wonderland, lightly.** One reference per slide is plenty. "Curiouser and curiouser" has been
  used (slide 3) — don't use it again.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Deep space | `#14123A` | Dark backgrounds, title text on light slides |
| Nebula purple | `#3B2A6B` | Secondary dark, chart series 2 |
| Espresso | `#6B4226` | Coffee, chart series 1 |
| Crema | `#F6EBD9` | Light backgrounds |
| Teal orbit | `#2BB3A3` | Highlights, "good" numbers |
| Heart red | `#D7263D` | The Queen, risks, warnings |
| Pocket-watch gold | `#F2B33D` | Accents, call-outs |

## Type

- **Headings:** Georgia, bold — storybook feel.
- **Body:** Arial — plain and legible on any machine.
- Sizes: titles 36–44 pt, body 18–24 pt, footnotes 11–12 pt.

## Pictures

- Illustrations live in `images/`: drawn in flat vector style with soft gradients — round shapes,
  a little sparkle, never photoreal.
- Characters appear as round portraits (see the team slide). The Cheshire Cat is always just a grin
  and eyes.
- Charts use Espresso, Nebula purple and Teal orbit, in that order; Heart red only for risk.

## Do / don't

- **Do** put the one number that matters in big type.
- **Do** keep a speaker note on every slide — the agent reads them.
- **Don't** use more than one character portrait per slide outside the team slide.
- **Don't** call the product a "cup" in titles — it's the **Drink Me Pod**.
